Crisis Support Centers

Local Crisis Hotlines
Most cities have local crisis hotlines. Search online: "[Your City] + crisis hotline" for local numbers.
Hospital Emergency Departments
If you're experiencing a mental health emergency, visit the nearest hospital emergency room. Staff are trained in mental health crisis response.
Mobile Crisis Units
Some areas have mobile crisis teams that can come to your location. Call local mental health services to inquire.
Psychiatric Urgent Care
Urgent care clinics with psychiatric services. Less wait time than ER for non-emergency crises.
Peer Support Groups
Local peer support groups meet regularly. SafeSpace community can help you find groups for your specific needs.

Warning Signs of Crisis

  • Talking about death or suicide
  • Intense hopelessness or worthlessness
  • Saying goodbye or giving away belongings
  • Major mood changes or emotional numbness
  • Severe anxiety or panic attacks
  • Reckless behavior or substance abuse increase

Immediate Action Steps

  1. Call 988 or your local crisis line
  2. Reach out to a trusted friend or family member
  3. Remove access to means of self-harm
  4. Go to the nearest emergency room
  5. Contact your therapist or psychiatrist
  6. Use SafeSpace chat support to talk immediately
