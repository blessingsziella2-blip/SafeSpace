Login Help

What if I forget my password?
Click on "Forgot your password?" below the login form. We'll send you a reset link to your email address.
Is my login information secure?
Yes! We use industry-standard encryption (SSL/TLS) to protect your data. Your password is never stored in plain text.
What does "Remember me" do?
If checked, we'll save your email address on this device. You'll still need to enter your password for security.
Can I login from multiple devices?
Yes! You can access your SafeSpace account from any device. Just log in with your credentials.
