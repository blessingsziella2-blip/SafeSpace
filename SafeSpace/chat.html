Chat Support Guidelines

Our Approach

  • ✅ Non-judgmental and supportive
  • ✅ Confidential and private
  • ✅ Available 24/7
  • ✅ Trained peer counselors and AI support

How to Get the Most Out of Chat

  • Be honest about how you're feeling
  • Take your time expressing yourself
  • Remember this is support, not a replacement for therapy
  • For emergencies, call 988 immediately

Not an Emergency Service

If you or someone you know is in immediate danger, please call 911 or visit the nearest emergency room. Chat support is for emotional support and wellness conversations, not crisis intervention.
